Data & privacy

Where the knowledge comes from

MedPeek doesn't guess from scratch. Before answering, it retrieves the most similar cases from a library built from open, expert-labeled datasets (FracAtlas, OrthoFrac-XR, BTXRD, BDCXR-3257, Indiana University chest X-rays, ROCOv2, Mammo-MX and more) and shows Gemma 4 those cases next to your image. Care-finder results come from US government data: the CMS hospital list, HIFLD hospital locations, the NPI Registry of licensed clinicians and GeoNames ZIP codes.

What happens to your image

MedPeek doesn't save your images. Names, IDs and other metadata are stripped before analysis. For fast answers, the image is sent to Google's Gemma 4 service, which has its own privacy terms; please don't upload images that show your name or ID. Your conversations are stored only in your MedPeek account.

MedPeek is AI and can make mistakes. It is a learning project, not a medical device, and not a diagnosis.
